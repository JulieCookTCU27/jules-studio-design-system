# Jules Studio — Design System

Part of the Cook Consulting Services, LLC brand family. Files: `tokens.json` (source of truth), `tokens.css` (CSS variables + font faces), the font files, logos, icons and images (all in the top level).

Jules Studio is Julie's handmade Etsy shop (JulesStudioTX, Dallas, Texas): custom floral letters and monograms built from silk blooms. The brand is the softest member of the family — the same linen ground, black line florals and League Spartan / Quicksand pairing as CCS, with colors taken from the flowers themselves: **plum, rose and blush**.

## Content fundamentals

- Voice: warm, personal, crafted. First person is fine — "I build every letter by hand." Speak to the gift-giver: "for her nursery", "for graduation day".
- Listing titles: product, letter/word, colors, occasion — "Floral Letter J — Lavender & Plum Silk Roses — Nursery Decor".
- Lowercase League Spartan for display lines (`display`, `h1`); `script` only for a signature word ("jules studio", "thank you").
- Tracked uppercase `label` for small banners: HANDCRAFTED BY · MADE TO ORDER · DALLAS, TX.
- Emoji: none in brand graphics; at most one in a social caption.

## Visual foundations

### Color

- Ground is `linen`; line art and text in `black`. Color comes from the product photos first, then from `plum`, `rose` and `blush`.
- `rose` (accent) for prices, sale tags and buttons; `plum` (accent-deep) for colored headings and footer bands; `blush` for soft fills behind text in `black` or `plum`.
- `peacock-blue` appears only as the quiet family link (e.g. footer: "a Cook Consulting Services brand").
- Evening theme: deep plum-black ground `#1E1520`, linen ink, light rose accent `#E8909E`.

### Type

- League Spartan for headings, Quicksand for body — the family pair.
- Sacramento (`script`) is a free monoline script chosen to sit with the badge's handwriting; it is not the badge's exact font. Use it sparingly.
- `label` mirrors the badge's spaced capitals.

### Layout

- Product photos on white or linen, square tiles (`radius-none`) in a grid with `space-4` gutters.
- The oval badge suggests one soft shape: `radius-pill` is allowed here for tags and buttons, unlike the other brands.
- 1px `rule` frames in `black`, like the badge's double oval. No shadows.

### Imagery

- Product photography: the letter centered on pure white, shot straight on, full letter visible, even soft light.
- The line florals (magnolia, wildflower stems, ginkgo, peony) and the shop banner's orchids frame graphics — black ink on linen, bleeding off edges.

- `jules-studio-badge-transparent.png`: the same, background removed, ink #101010 — for placing on linen, blush or photos with light backgrounds.
- `jules-etsy-banner.jpg`: the shop banner (1680×420): "HANDCRAFTED BY / jules studio" between orchid line drawings.

## Iconography

No icon set. Use the floral line drawings as ornament; for shop UI, thin-line icons (1.5px) in `ink`.

## The family

Cook Consulting Services, LLC is the house; each business below is its own brand with its own system, cut from the same cloth.

| Brand | Role | What it keeps from CCS | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CCS | Parent consultancy | — | — |
| The Collective Space | Community and gathering brand | Palette, League Spartan, Quicksand, florals | Leads on black; script word in the logo |
| Impact Advisory Group | Advisory practice | Palette, League Spartan, Quicksand, florals | Quietest of the set: ink and linen, one accent |
| Red Tape Academy | Education DBA | Linen, peacock blue, sky blue, black, Quicksand, League Spartan labels | Adds civic red; Libre Baskerville headings; no florals |
| Jules Studio | Handmade Etsy shop | Linen, black, Quicksand, League Spartan, line florals | Plum, rose and blush from the floral work |

Threads every brand keeps, so the family reads as one: the linen ground (`linen` #F1EFE7), black ink (`black` #101010), Quicksand for body copy, League Spartan somewhere in the hierarchy, wide-tracked lowercase or uppercase sub-lines (as in "a d v i s o r y   g r o u p"), square corners, and 1px hairline rules framing the page.
