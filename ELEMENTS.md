# Elements

Line-drawn florals from the CCS brand book: magnolia, peony, ginkgo line and wildflower stems.

- `floral-*-wash.png` — each floral with a loose lavender and/or sky watercolor wash behind it, transparent, ink #101010. The Jules Studio version; one or two per layout.
- `floral-*.png` — the plain black-ink originals (#101010), transparent; use where a product photo already brings color.

Both are images, so they can't be recolored in a page: place them on `linen`, white or `wash-warm` / `wash-cool`. Ask Canva for the vector originals for large print.
