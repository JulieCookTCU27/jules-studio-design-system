# Elements

Line-drawn florals from the brand book: magnolia, wildflower stems, ginkgo line and peony. Transparent PNGs, single ink `#101010` (black) — an `<img>` cannot recolor them, so place them on `linen` or another light ground. One or two per layout. They were cut from the brand book at high resolution; ask Canva for the original vector files for large print.
